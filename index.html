      The circuit

      A lap of the canyon

      The road leaves the gantry flat out, kinks back on itself through the infield and comes home on one long sweep under the mesas.

      Run wide onto the sand and you lose more than half your top speed. The walls behind the sand do not move.

      • Start and finish
      • Boost pad, three per lap
      • Sand run-off

      There is a second circuit too. Rattlesnake Pass is longer and has more corners to get wrong.

      The grid

      Pick a driver

      Six of them, none sensible. Choose one and the other five line up ahead of you, quickest first. The slowest still runs at 89% of your top speed.

      Sit behind one and they move over to cover the inside. They slide through the tight corners, and now and then one brakes too late and runs wide.

      Whoever gets a long way ahead of you eases off a touch. Whoever drops a long way back finds a little more.

        Driving

        Hold it. Let go.

        Hold Space through a corner and the back steps out. Keep it sliding for more than half a second, let go, and whatever you put in the meter comes back as a boost. The longer the slide, the bigger the boost: the smoke turns yellow, then red, to tell you.

        Yellow boxes hand out one of three things. A Nitro, a Tumbleweed to drop behind you, or a Stinger that goes after the kart in front.

        • WAccelerate
        • SBrake, then reverse
        • A   DSteer
        • SpaceDrift
        • EUse an item
        • RGo again

        Arrow keys work too. On a phone the throttle holds itself and you get steer, brake and drift buttons.
